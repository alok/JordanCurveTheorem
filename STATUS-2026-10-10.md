## What I changed

One commit, `8d8b3ad` "Tighten infinitesimal and saturation wording in the explainer".
It only touches `docs/explainer.md`:

- **Glossary, "Infinitesimal":** the old text said "smaller than every ordinary positive
  number", which literally includes −5. It now says "smaller in absolute value…; zero is
  one, but there are nonzero ones too."
- **§5 intro:** infinitesimals were described as "positive numbers smaller than every
  positive real". The new wording, "nonzero numbers whose size is smaller…", matches the
  glossary and covers negative infinitesimals, which the old wording left out.
- **§5 `mem_deep_iff` bullet:** the saturation sentence dropped the key condition. It now
  says the saturated point is *outside the internal set*, which is why `a` would not be
  deep.
- **§2 Osgood bullet:** removed a redundant restatement of "positive area".
